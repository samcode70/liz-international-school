Admission Process

Simple Admission Process

Joining Liz International School is easy. Follow these simple steps to begin your child's educational journey.

1

Application

Obtain and complete the school's admission form.

2

Assessment

Applicants may take an entrance assessment appropriate for their class.

3

Interview

Parents and pupils may be invited for a brief interaction with the school.

4

Admission

Successful applicants receive an admission offer and enrolment details.

Requirements

Admission Requirements

To ensure a smooth admission process, parents are encouraged to prepare the following documents before applying.

Birth Certificate

A photocopy of the child's birth certificate or age declaration.

Previous School Result

Applicable to pupils transferring from another school.

Passport Photographs

Recent passport photographs of the pupil.

Medical Information

Any relevant medical records or special healthcare information.

Parent Information

Valid contact details of parents or guardians.

Completed Application Form

Submit the school's admission form with all required information.
