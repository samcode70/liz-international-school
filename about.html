Our Story

Building Leaders Through Quality Education

Liz International School was established with a vision of providing quality education that develops every child's academic ability, character and leadership potential. We believe education extends beyond the classroom and should prepare learners for lifelong success.

Located at Giwa Oke Aro, Ogun State, our school offers Nursery, Primary and Secondary education in a safe, disciplined and stimulating learning environment where every child is encouraged to excel.

Guided by our motto, "We Build Leaders", we continue to nurture confident, responsible and innovative learners who are equipped to make meaningful contributions to society.

Our Mission

To produce:

  • Total youths.
  • Youths that are academically and socially successful.
  • Youths that are spiritually, physically and morally balanced.

To achieve the above, qualified teachers are employed, practical excursions are organized, extracurricular activities are provided, and laboratories are effectively utilized.
